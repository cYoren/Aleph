Read the Bible in the words it was written in.

Aleph puts the Hebrew Scriptures and the Greek New Testament in your hands with everything you need to actually read them: every word pronounced, defined and parsed, one tap away. Start from the alphabet or open straight to your favorite passage.

TAP ANY WORD
• Full lexicon entry from Brown Driver Briggs (Hebrew) and Abbott Smith (Greek)
• Complete parsing: stem, tense, person, number, gender, case
• Transliteration above every word, so you can sound it out from day one
• Every occurrence of the word across the Bible, one tap away

HEAR IT
• Every verse read aloud in Hebrew or Greek
• Follow along as each verse lights up while it is read
• Masoretic accents shown as sense units, so long verses break into phrases you can follow

LEARN IT FOR GOOD

• Spaced repetition brings each word back right before you would forget it
• A reading map shows how much of each book you can already read on your own
• Parsing quizzes and Psalm memorization for when you want to go deeper
• An alphabet guide for Hebrew and Greek letters, vowels and sounds

STUDY LIKE A SCHOLAR
• 340,000+ cross references

• Read in Tanakh order (Torah, Nevi'im, Ketuvim) or in Christian canon order
• Bookmarks and beautiful verse images to share
